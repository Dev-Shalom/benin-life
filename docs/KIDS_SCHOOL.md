# Kids school and university (step B of the family plan)

Owner plan: "School for kids" and the owner's addition "University" (family-life-plan.html, 25 October). Migration `supabase/migrations/20261029000100_kids_school.sql`, test `supabase/tests/kids_school_test.sql` (and `kids_test`, `kids_v2_test` updated for the new stage and items). Client: `src/screens/game/phone/SchoolPanel.tsx` (School panel, enrol picker, WAEC card, university card, report cards with Share), `kidsShared.tsx`, `KidsSection.tsx`, `src/api/kids.ts`. It reuses the life-moments engine (docs/MOMENTS.md) for school requests and the university decision, and the kids system (docs/LOVE.md) for needs, spend and the gift. The player school (courses, certificates: docs/SCHOOL.md) is a separate system.

**Nothing here touches `profiles`** (no alter, trigger or foreign key; the new tables have no foreign keys at all). No new cron, no new realtime, no global scan: the next school moment of a child lives in `kids_children.next_alert_at` (indexed `kids_children_due`), so the existing sweep (`bl_push_tick` -> `bl_kids_alert_sweep`) and the lazy `kids_status()` read are enough.

## The rules as built (every number is a Setting, category `kids_school`; the Graduate gift is `kids.gift_graduate`)
1. **Schools** (`kids_schools`, Admin -> Content -> Schools): 6 fictional schools and 2 universities. Nursery + Primary: Uselu Model Nursery & Primary School (budget, 20,000, +0 %), Third East Bright Stars School (mid, 55,000, +5 %), GRA Royal Kids Academy (premium, 120,000, +12 %). Secondary: Ugbowo Grammar School (budget, 25,000), Ikpoba Hill Comprehensive College (mid, 60,000, +5 %), Sapele Road Unity Secondary (premium, 150,000, +12 %). University (fee per level): UNIBEN (public, 60,000, +4 %), Edo Royal University (private, 400,000, +14 %). Columns: name, icon, tier, levels, area (a label), fee per class, quality (grade boost %), blurb, active, sort. Names are checked by the banned-words list. No map pins: enrolment is from the Family app.
2. **Classes**, 14 in `kids_classes`: Nursery 1-2, Primary 1-6, JSS 1-3, SSS 1-3. One class lasts `class_hours` (24). A class never starts before its life stage does (Nursery day 5, Primary 8, Secondary 14), so a child enrolled on time gets Nursery on days 5-7, Primary on 8-14, JSS 1 to SSS 3 on 14-20 and WAEC on day 20; one day of slack in Nursery and Secondary, none in Primary. The school clock is independent of the 21-day growth clock: a child who repeats may not reach SSS 3 before day 21; the outcome says how far they got.
3. **Enrol**: `kids_school_enrol(child, school)`, either parent. The school must teach the level of the child's current class (a child enrolled for the first time starts at the first class of their current stage; a baby is "too young"). Switching school is allowed until the current class fee is paid; when the next class needs a level the school does not teach (Uselu has no secondary) the child waits and both parents are told to pick one (`kids_school_pick`).
4. **Fee**: `kids_school_pay(child)`, either parent, ledger `kid_school_fee` (a sink, never earnings, category "School fees"), counts as spend. Paid within `fee_grace_hours` (12) of the class start = on time (+points). A reminder alert comes half way. **Unpaid when the class ends = the child missed it**: every subject starts from `missed_grade` (22), so the child repeats, and loses `missed_fun_hit` Fun (and half that Love): sad.
5. **Grades** (per subject 0-100; Nursery: Numbers, Letters, Play; Primary: Maths, English, Basic Science, Social Studies; Secondary: Maths, English, Biology/Physics, Economics, Civic Education; University: Course work, Exams; Content -> School subjects). For an attended class: `base_grade` 38 + fee on time 6 + needs (share of the class the child's needs were above the alert level, from -12 to +12) + homework 4 per parent help + textbooks 5 + bag and uniform 3 + extra lessons 8 - penalties + a fixed hidden **talent** per child and subject (+/- `talent_pts` 8, deterministic from the child id), then x (1 + school quality %), capped 0-100. A child with fees paid on time and a cared-for child averages 56 before talent; two homework helps add 8. The needs share is taken from the same care-score integral the gift uses, snapshotted at class start (`good0`, `total0`).
6. **Helps**: `kids_school_homework` (once per class per parent, `homework_cooldown_minutes` 60 shared), `kids_school_lessons` (a tutor for one class, `lessons_cost` 8,000, ledger `kid_school_lessons`), `kids_school_gear(child, books|uniform)` (uses the item from the bag: Textbooks (primary) 9,500, Textbooks (secondary) 18,000, School bag & uniform 12,000, sold at Oba Market and the supermarket with the other kids items; textbooks last for the whole level, the uniform for one stage; the nominal price counts as spend).
7. **Promotion**: class average >= `pass_mark` (50) and attended = promoted; else the child repeats the class (every try is kept). Highest class reached drives the outcome line.
8. **School requests** (life moments of kind `kids_school`, both parents get the card): PTA levy 6,000, excursion 9,000, sports wear 7,500, end-of-term party 4,000, WAEC fee 25,000 (always in SSS 3). `request_pct` (60) chance per class, at most one per child per class (`kids_school_requests` unique child + class + attempt), planned for 15-65 % into the class, **skipped when either parent is at the daily moments cap** (retried every 3 hours; the WAEC card is exempt). Pay (ledger `life_moment`, counts as spend; the first parent to pay settles it and the other card becomes "Already handled by @x"), Later (the normal snooze) or Ignore (a free choice). The grade penalty (`ignore_penalty` 4 on every subject of that class) and the child's sadness (`ignore_fun_hit` 15) apply once **both** parents have ignored it (or let the card expire). A request left open at the end of the class closes with no penalty.
9. **Report cards** (`kids_report_cards`): at the end of Nursery, Primary, JSS, SSS and University: subjects with grades (the average of the final try of each class of the stage), average, position ("Excellent" 75+, "Very good" 60+, "Good" 50+, "Needs help"), a warm remark and a fictional teacher (Mrs. Efe Aghedo, Mr. Osaretin Aigbe ...) picked by child, promoted or not. The SSS card carries the WAEC line, the university card the degree. A child who leaves in the middle of a stage still gets a "Left school during this stage" card. Share (client): `navigator.share` when it exists, else the text is copied; there is no public page.
10. **WAEC** (after SSS 3 is passed): nine papers (`kids_subjects`, level `waec`), each from the SSS 3 grade of its secondary subject +/- talent. A credit is a paper at or above `waec_credit_mark` (50, the real C6; letters A1 to F9). **Pass = at least `waec_pass_credits` (5) credits including Mathematics and English Language**: "7 credits including Maths and English"; fail: "fewer than 5 credits" (or "N credits but no credit in Maths and English"). An ignored WAEC fee card takes 6 points off every paper.
11. **University** (`uni_enabled`): on day 21, a child with a WAEC pass does not move out. Status `university` (not counted as an active child, no needs alerts), and **both parents get "Send {child} to university?"**: UNIBEN, Edo Royal University (the fee per level is shown in the choice) or "Let them start working" (the card's free ignore choice; no answer in `uni_decision_hours` (36) = work). The first answer settles it. Levels 100L to 400L, `uni_level_hours` (24) each; fee per level (either parent, same `kid_school_fee`, unpaid = level missed); Course work and Exams grades; promoted at the pass mark; a failed level repeats, **at most `uni_max_repeats` (2) repeats in total, the next failure means dropping out** ("Didn't finish university"). Needs are simplified: pocket money (`uni_pocket_cost` 6,000 cash, the Feed action) and attention (Cuddle); no diapers, toys, medicine or neglect. Course (Medicine, Law, Engineering ...) is picked by the child id.
12. **Graduation**: average of the final try of the four levels -> First Class (>= 70), Second Class Upper (>= 60), Second Class Lower (>= 55), Third Class. The child moves out with the new top tier **Graduate** (above Gold) and the gift `kids.gift_graduate` (250,000 each parent, ledger `kid_gift`, never earnings).
13. **Outcome line** (stored on the child, shown in the Family tab, in the keepsake alert): "Graduated from UNIBEN with Second Class Upper in Medicine", "Got 7 credits in WAEC and started work", "Sat WAEC but did not get enough credits (3)", "Didn't finish secondary school (reached JSS 2)", "Didn't finish primary school (reached Primary 3)", "Only went as far as Nursery 2", "Didn't finish university (Edo Royal University, Law)", "Moved out without going to school".
14. **Gift cap**: still `kids.gift_max_spend_pct` (25 %) of what that parent spent on the child, and now the spend includes school fees, request payments, lessons, textbooks, bag and uniform (all through `bl_kids_spent`). The full tier amounts become reachable for parents who invest (a Gold child whose parent paid ~400,000 in fees gets the whole 100,000); a parent who paid nothing gets nothing.
15. **Switches**: `kids_school.enabled` off = no enrolment, payment or help and no class starts or ends (classes that run out meanwhile are marked when it is back on; the kids kill switch `kids.enabled` also shifts the school clocks); `kids_school.uni_enabled` off = a WAEC pass just moves out.

## Decisions to confirm with the owner
- Textbooks, bag and uniform are given from the bag (buy them at the shop first, like the care supplies); there is no remote buying.
- A university child is not counted as an active child (a couple can have another baby), gets no needs alerts and cannot be neglected; only pocket money and attention.
- The Graduate gift replaces the care-tier gift (paid once, at graduation); a drop-out or a child who chose work gets the normal Gold/Silver/Bronze tier.
- WAEC is sat only after SSS 3 is passed (a failed SSS 3 repeats and WAEC waits).
- An ignored school request needs both parents to ignore it before the child pays the price.
