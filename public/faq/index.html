Benin Life: frequently asked questions

Who is the owner of Benin Life?

Benin Life was created and is owned by Shalom Uwagboe, a game creator from Benin City, Nigeria. Read more about the creator and the game.

What is Benin Life?

Benin Life is a free online life game set in Benin City, Edo State, Nigeria. You work, run a business, go to school, raise a family and chat with other players.

Is Benin Life free?

Yes. Benin Life is free to play. There is nothing to download.

How do I play Benin Life?

Open playbeninlife.app in your phone or computer browser, sign up and create your character. It works on any modern browser.

Play Benin Life free

Do I need to download an app?

No. Benin Life runs in your browser. You can add it to your phone home screen from the browser menu.

Who can play Benin Life?

Players must be 18 or older.
