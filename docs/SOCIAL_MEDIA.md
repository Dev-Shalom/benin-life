Owner: Shalom (posts). Claude: writes the post packs, reminds daily, and adds a pack after every update.
Main channel: TikTok @beninlife (Benin Life Official). Also post to Instagram Reels, X and a WhatsApp Channel.
Link in bio: playbeninlife.app (use `?ref=CODE` so referrals count).

## 1. The big rule: TikTok loves video, not posters
AI images are fine for announcements, but **screen recordings of real gameplay get 5–20× more views**.
- Record on your phone: iPhone Control Centre → Screen Recording; Android → quick settings → Screen recorder.
- Keep it 7–20 seconds, hook in the first second, a trending sound under it, and big captions on screen.
- Use the image prompts below for thumbnails, carousels and "update posters".

## 5. Growth tactics (to get a bigger community)
- **Go live on TikTok** 2× a week and play with viewers: elections, club nights, gang jobs. Live builds loyal fans fastest.
- **Benin creators**: send 5–10 Benin TikTokers (5k–50k followers) a free in-game boost or a small fee to play on camera. One good creator video can bring hundreds of players.
- **Referral contest**: "Invite 5 friends with your link → ₦500k in-game + shout-out". Every player already has a referral link (Hustles app → Invite & earn).
- **WhatsApp Channel + group**: post every update there; ask UNIBEN/UNIBEN-area groups to share.
- **Reply to every comment** in the first hour after posting (the algorithm notices).
- **Hashtags** (use 3–5): #BeninLife #BeninCity #Edo #Naija #LagosLife #LifeSimulator #NigerianGames #UNIBEN #Benin #Gaming
- **Same time every day** (7–9 PM) so followers learn when you post.
