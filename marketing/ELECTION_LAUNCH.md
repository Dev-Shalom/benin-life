## How the election works (plain words)
One election fills every seat at once. It runs in 4 steps:

| Step | How long | What players do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Registration | 2 days | Declare: pick a seat, write a promise (10-140 letters), pick a party or go independent, pay the fee |
| 2. Campaign | 3 days | Rallies, posters, supporters and endorsements build **momentum** |
| 3. Voting day | 1 day | Go to **your ward's polling unit** on the map and vote once per seat |
| 4. Results | at the close | Winners are sworn in for **14 days**: title, daily salary, powers |

After this first one, the next election starts by itself, so new results land when the term ends.

Everyone: not in jail, and no EFCC (Yahoo) case in the last 7 days. One race per player.
Salaries per day: Councillor ₦10k, Chairman ₦25k, Governor ₦50k, Commissioner ₦15k.

Wards: Ugbowo, Uselu, Ring Road, GRA, Sapele Rd, New Benin, Ikpoba Hill, Airport Rd. Your home's area decides your ward.

## Campaigning
- **Rally** (₦20,000): hold it in a public place. Every player standing there adds points, so a crowd beats money. 20 min cooldown, 6 a day.
- **Poster** (₦10,000): small points, up to 5.
- **Support** (free, anonymous), **Endorse** (named, needs Charisma 2), **Gang endorsement** (Boss or Lieutenant, worth the most).
- **Momentum bar**: Leading / Close behind / Chasing / Cold. It never shows vote counts.
- **Vote buying is not allowed.** Every candidate card has a Report button. Admin can disqualify.

## Voting
- Polling units open on voting day only. Stand at your own ward's unit and tap **Vote here**.
- Account must be 3 days old. One vote per seat. Votes are secret, even from the admin, until the close.
- Ties: most rally points wins, then a coin flip. A lone candidate wins even with 0 votes.

## Winner powers
- **Governor:** appoints and sacks the 6 Commissioners, sends one Government announcement a day to everyone, and picks **one policy** for the term:
  - Keke Fridays: keke 30% cheaper on Friday
  - Market levy holiday: fewer council levies on businesses
  - Youth sports week: +25% Fitness XP
  - Clean Benin: hygiene drops slower
- **Councillor:** one notice to their ward.
- Every winner gets a title badge next to their name (Hon., Chairman, Gov., Comm.).
