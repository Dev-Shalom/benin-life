# Free roam (walk and drive the whole city in 3D)

Opt-in third-person roaming in the real 3D streets of Benin City. Nothing changes for players who never use it. Phase 1 walked the King's Square box. **Phase 2** (this document) opens the **whole city map**: streamed chunks, collision everywhere, your own car, hailing a keke, a search for the GPS line, the closed airport.

Look: the same calm, clean, flat-shaded low-poly as the 3D map and the drive scene, at human scale. Procedural geometry only, theme tokens for the UI (Light and Dark), no real brands.

## Entry and exit
- **Step outside** (home chip row) and **Walk the city** (map view row): on any active place (the whole city is open, setting `roam.area.all`). Calls `roam_start`, the street opens at the place's door. With `roam.area.all` off the old King's Square box applies and **Go to King's Square to walk** returns.
- **Go in** at a door: `roam_enter(place)`. On success the game refreshes, free roam closes and the place opens exactly as today. **Leave** returns to the map.
- **Hail keke** and the car button work only inside the street. **Quick travel** from the map stays available everywhere.

## Controls and UI (kept out of the way)
Touch: joystick bottom-left, drag anywhere else to orbit, Walk / Run (Brake while driving), the contextual action (Go in / Talk), the **car button** (Get my car / Get in / Get out), top-right: Leave, view, **search** (destination for the GPS line anywhere in the city), **Hail keke** (appears when you stand on a pavement beside a road). Keyboard: WASD or arrows, Shift run, Space brake, E / Enter action, G car, V view, M map, R run toggle.
Driving: stick up = go, down = brake then reverse, left / right = steer; the Brake button is the hand brake. Chase view, or **Driver view** with the drive scene's dashboard (steering wheel that turns, speed, fuel gauge, warning lights, wipers in the rain).

## Streaming design
- The city (1000 x 1000 m map, 81 chunks of 120 m, `roam.chunk.m`) is built **at most a few milliseconds a frame**: `buildChunkGen` is a generator that yields every few buildings / road segments / one attribute copy; the scheduler (`stream.ts`) runs it for 3 ms a frame on Low and 4 ms on High (+2 ms above 12 m/s, 14 ms while the first chunks load). Next chunk = nearest to the player with a bias of up to 55 m for where you are heading.
- **Detail radius** `roam.chunk.load_m` (200 m on High, 150 m on Low = 75 %): chunks whose nearest edge is inside it are built; they are disposed 40 m beyond it (geometry only, materials, the sign atlas and the instanced meshes are shared). Fog is 60-340 m (High) / 45-270 m (Low) so nothing pops in view.
- **Backdrop**: beyond the detail radius up to 1.75x of it, each chunk is ONE merged mesh of plain box buildings, road ribbons and ground (one draw call, built in a couple of milliseconds). A chunk shows its backdrop until its detailed mesh is in.
- One chunk is built with the Phase 1 code (windows, doors, awnings, shop fronts, signs, lamps, props, landmark models) but visits only the items listed in it (`chunks.ts`) and keeps only the triangles whose centre lies in the chunk (`Soup.clip`), so a building on a border is split cleanly: no seams, no duplicates. The soup writes into growable typed arrays with flat normals computed on the way (no per-frame garbage, no `computeVertexNormals`).
- **Trees, tree shadows and lamp pools** are three shared instanced meshes refilled when a chunk comes or goes; the camera fades trees between you and the lens (`eachTreeNear`).
- Before the first frame the 3x3 chunks round the player are built behind the "Stepping out" screen.
- **Collision is not chunked.** The whole city's static colliders (about 2,300 polygons, 10,200 edges, 1,600 posts and trunks: a few hundred KB) live in one spatial hash built once with the world (about 1.5 s on a desktop, behind the loading screen), so the set around the player is always complete before the player can reach it, at any speed. Only meshes stream.

### Districts and look
Heights, palettes and crowds follow the city's districts (`drive3d/district.ts`): the centre grows to 4-5 floors, GRA is low white villas under terracotta / green / slate roofs behind compound walls with a gate gap and yard trees, UNIBEN / UBTH campus is long low cream blocks under green roofs, markets and crowded quarters stay small and rusty, the outskirts are huts. Markets: up to 44 stalls (Oba Market) with sellers and umbrellas, signed entrance arches. Motor parks: a yellow entrance arch and **danfo minibuses** (deep body, black waist band, glazed cabin, roof rack) on the pad. Every place has an entrance: a door with awning and sign on a building (or a small shop put up beside the road), an arch (museum, markets, motor parks, campus, shrine, farm, stadium) or a kiosk (PoS stands, street spots). Every other building gets a door; some on the main roads are small shop fronts with made-up names; lamps every ~21 m with warm pools at night.
**Benin Airport** is present but closed: a shut steel gate between posts, a red and white barrier, its name and an **"Under construction"** board. Walking up to it shows "Go in"; the server refuses (`inactive`: "Benin Airport is under construction. Check back soon.").
Crowd: walkers recycle round the player (never in front of the camera close up) with a density per district (market 1.0 ... GRA 0.18, outskirts 0.07); sellers and idlers move to free stalls / doors near you.

## Your own car
- **Rules as the drive scene**: car, SUV, pickup, motorcycle or bicycle you own (`vehicleFor`, the primary vehicle `bl_driving_item`), same models (`vehicleKit`: body, steering and spinning wheels, brake lights), same cockpit and dashboard components, headlight cones and tail lights at night, wipers and rain streaks from the server's daily rain schedule (`bl_drive_weather`), condition and fuel chips.
- **Get my car**: it appears at the nearest free kerb 8 to 45 m away (parallel to the road, clear of colliders, never at a junction, never within 8 m of any person or traffic car, fades in over 0.7 s); a blue beam marks it. **Get in** at the driver door (left side, within 3 m) calls `roam_set_mode('car')` (the server checks ownership, condition above 0, fuel above 0); **Get out** puts you beside the driver door (or the other side / the rear) and records where the car stands (`roam_set_mode('walk', x, y, a)`). Pulling up at a place by car leaves the car at its kerb for the next walk. It is also found where you left it.
- **Park anywhere legal**: the car must be nearly stopped and within about a metre of the carriageway edge or off the road (`parkLegal`); "You cannot leave it in the middle of the road".
- **Physics** (`carphys.ts`): kinematic bicycle model (steering angle tightens with speed, tops out at 20 m/s on a sedan, 6.5 m/s on a bicycle, slower off the road, less grip in rain), body roll into the turn, nose dip under braking, wheels spin with the real distance. Fixed 60 Hz steps.
- **Collision**: the body is a set of circles (big ones down the middle, rows of small ones along nose and tail, corner discs) moved in **sub-steps of at most 5 cm**, pushed out of walls, poles, trunks, parked cars (statics) and traffic (boxes). A step that cannot be resolved returns to the last valid pose. Impacts above 1.6 m/s count as **light**, above 6.5 m/s as **hard** (0.8 s cooldown); the car loses speed, bounces a little, the camera shakes. No tunnelling: the stress test checks every step.
- **Pedestrians are a soft collider**: they step aside early (before a moving car is within 4 m + 1.1 s of travel, up to 3.6 m/s), and the car is blocked 0.7 m before touching one (no damage, no contact). NPC cars brake for your car (three circles) like for a person, keep a gap in which they could stop dead, and **never fade in within 8 m of a person or your car** (they wait unseen at the end of their stretch, braking smoothly, or are held at the start). This also fixes the Phase 1 rough edge where a car fading in could shove a standing player.
- **Fuel and damage**: the gauge falls with the distance driven (client estimate: litres per km of the vehicle x `roam.drive.fuel_scale`), and every ~4 s `roam_drive_report` sends the metres, the light / hard hits, the position. The server clamps and applies (below) and answers with the real tank and condition. **0 fuel**: the car rolls to a stop and the **Out of fuel** card offers **Push the car to the kerb** (free), **Call a mechanic** (`drive.dry.*` settings: cash, litres, a short wait before the car starts again) or **Hitch a keke** (parks the car and opens the hail sheet at the normal fare). **Condition 0**: the car will not start ("Repair it at the Mechanic Village, or hail a keke").

## Hail a keke
On any pavement beside a road the **Hail keke** button appears (setting `roam.keke.enabled`). It opens the place list (distance from you, sorted, with search); picking one calls `roam_hail_keke(dest)`, a guarded wrapper round the existing `travel_start(dest, 'keke')`: normal fare, normal trip clock and keke rules (keke only between keke-friendly places), the existing 3D ride, then free roam opens again at the destination door (`roamPending`). The airport and other closed places are refused by travel. Not from inside your car.

## GPS search
The magnifier button lists every place in the city (name, kind, distance), searchable; picking one draws the green line and the beam. On foot the line follows pavements and crossings (A* on a 1 m grid of the colliders); in the car the line is wider. The mini-map opens to the whole city; tap a pin for the same line.

## Collision design (people)
- Statics: polygons as edge segments with outward normals and **thickness** (a thin compound wall or gate is never treated as "deep inside" from its far side; this also fixed a phase 1 tunnelling case at tiny OSM slabs), posts and trunks as circles, in a dense hash (CSR arrays, no allocation per query).
- The player is a circle (r 0.32 m); `move()` uses sub-steps of at most 0.4 x radius and pushes out of overlaps; motion along a wall survives (slides); a squeezed step returns to the last valid spot.
- Camera collision: a height-aware ray from the body (walking), pulled in by buildings in the chase cam.

### Stress test (`scripts/roam-collide-check.mjs`, whole city, seed 424242)
1. **Static**: 1,750,000 random moves (walk, run, 9, 14, 30, 60 m/s, hitch frames to 0.25 s, aimed at walls and corners) from random spots all over the city: smallest clearance 0.319 m (radius 0.32), inside a collider 0, outside the city 0; sliding along walls keeps 100 % of free speed, no backward jitter.
2. **Cars and people**: 260 cars, 27 pedestrians, 240 s, scripted players working among the cars: 0 player / car overlaps, 0 car / car overlaps, 0 pedestrians inside colliders, fastest push 4.9 m/s (no shoves).
3. **Doors and GPS**: all 40 places' standing spots are clear and reachable (a final pass moves a spot to the nearest reachable free cell when later colliders sealed a pocket); 1,560 routes between all pairs, none unreachable, none through a collider.
4. **Driving**: 260 drives (sedan, SUV, pickup, motorcycle, bicycle) over the whole city, 468,000 fixed steps, 16,800 m, up to 17.5 m/s, aimed at walls and poles, with and without traffic, 4 standing pedestrians ahead on a quarter of the runs: 342 light and 168 hard hits; after EVERY step an independent exact test of the oriented box: **never inside a collider** (worst brush 4 cm at a corner), never outside the city, **no step jumps (0 tunnelling)**, never deep inside a traffic car, never within the soft margin of a pedestrian.
5. **Spawning**: 40 lane starts with a person standing there for 25 s: 398,000 fade-in checks, 0 cars appeared within 8 m (47,000 held-back car steps).

## Server (positions are cosmetic; money, fuel and damage are not)
`roam_sessions` (user_id key, no profiles column, no FK, no trigger, RLS on, no grants). Phase 1 RPCs: `roam_start`, `roam_enter`, `roam_leave` (see the migration header). Phase 2 (`20261119000100_roam2.sql`, live functions patched by guarded single replace through `pg_temp.roam2_patch`):
- **Area**: `bl_roam_in_area` is "any place" when `roam.area.all` is on, **except remote places** (`locations.remote_km > 0`, e.g. Iguobazuwa Farm: they stay quick-travel only; `roam_enter` and `roam_hail_keke` refuse them with `outside`, and the client leaves them out of the world). Inactive places are still refused by `roam_enter` (`inactive`, travel's own words; your own home is allowed). Off = the old box.
- `roam_set_mode(mode, vehicle, x, y, a)`: `walk` records where the vehicle was left (clamped to the map) and **always works** (no switch, busy, rate or session check: nobody is ever stuck in car mode); `car` checks `roam.drive.enabled`, ownership, overdue vehicle bills (`bl_sink_blocked`, hint `blocked`, as travel), condition (`wont_start`), fuel (`no_fuel`) and a mechanic or push still under way (`busy`), and counts against the rate window.
- `roam_enter` anti-teleport uses the mode: walking pace (`roam.max_speed_mps`) or `roam.drive.max_speed_mps` in the car, and in the car the session must have **reported at least `roam.drive.min_report_pct` % of the distance** through fuel checkpoints ("You have not driven that far yet"). Arriving by car applies the same hooks as travel (`bl_vip_arrival`, `bl_roll_street_robbery` with mode `car`, its own multiplier `crime.mode_mult_car`), no fare, and leaves the car at the place.
- `roam_drive_report({dist_m, light, hard, x, y, a})`: only way fuel burns and damage lands. Rate limits: at most one per `roam.drive.report_gap_s`, `roam.drive.max_reports_min` a minute. Distance: a JSON number, clamped to the **class pace** (`bl_roam_speed_cap`: bicycle `roam.drive.max_speed_bicycle` 7, motorcycle `roam.drive.max_speed_motorcycle` 22, everything at most `roam.drive.max_speed_mps` 24) x elapsed + `roam.drive.slack_m` x min(1, elapsed / 10 s) (the slack is per 10 s, not per report), then by the fuel in the tank (litres / litres per metre + 2 m), and to **2 m at most with an empty tank (0.01 L) or condition 0** (the credit never exceeds what the burn paid for). `roam_enter` uses the same class pace. Fuel burn: distance x `roam.drive.fuel_scale` x litres per km of the class (`drive.fuel.lpk_*`) x wear. Hits: counts, at most `roam.drive.max_hits_report` and elapsed / `roam.drive.min_s_per_hit` + 1; damage = light x `damage_light` + hard x `damage_hard` (x `drive.rain.damage_mult` in rain), clamped per report (`max_damage_report`) and per rolling minute (`max_damage_min`); condition floors at 0, the tank at 0; the answer carries `empty` / `wont_start`.
- `roam_dry_choose('push' | 'mechanic')`: out of fuel (not offered when `drive.fuel.enabled` is off). **Push** applies `drive.dry.push_s` and the `push_fail_pct` / `push_fail_s` roll on the server, stress, and a stranger spares `roam.dry.push_litres` (1.5 L) so the car can move again once the push time has passed; **mechanic** takes the cash and fills the tank after his wait. Client and server both call a tank dry at 0.01 L.
- `roam_hail_keke(dest)`, `roam_car_info()`.
Tests: `roam_test.sql` (phase 1, run with `roam.area.all` off) and `roam2_test.sql` (settings, whole city, closed airport, mode and ownership, driving anti-teleport, fuel validation and clamps, damage clamps, rate limits, robbery with mode car and no fare, out of fuel, keke, structure and grants). Time is pinned with `bl.test_offset_seconds`; street robbery is switched off or forced where money is asserted.

## Loading and look rules (review fixes)
- The first thing on screen is a **"Loading the city…"** card; the whole-city collision world (about 1.5 s) is built right after that first paint, and cached by geometry-relevant data only (ids, positions, kinds): a place's active flag flipping does not rebuild it (a closed gate is decided when the world is built). It is one synchronous build after the paint, not time-sliced or in a Worker.
- Getting in: the scene checks you are at the driver door first, then `roam_set_mode('car')`; if the server refuses you step back out. Getting out retries `roam_set_mode('walk')` once and says so if it still fails; the tank and condition are re-read from the server after a mechanic or push.
- **No thin towers**: a building's height may not exceed 1.5 x its narrower side (minimum 2.3 m) unless it is a real multi-storey (broad footprint, 7 m or more across, in the centre or on a main road); market zones are one floor at most; footprints under about 2.2 m across (OSM market stands) are low 1.3 m blocks. The audit finds 0 slabs taller than that rule. Shop and place signs sit on buildings tall enough to carry them (places get at least 3.8 m), and every sign board is lettered on both faces.

## Live players (phase 3)
Other real players in the street, walking or driving, with name tags, wave / emotes and chat bubbles. Server `20261120000100_roam3.sql`, tests `supabase/tests/roam3_test.sql`, client `src/art/roam3d/live/**` + `engine/others.ts`, load test `scripts/roam-load.mjs`, setting category "Free roam" (`roam.live.*`, `roam.bubble.*`, `roam.report.max_hour`).

### Security model
- **Two private broadcast topics per player**, both guarded by Realtime Authorization (policies on `realtime.messages`, all decided by `roam_topic_ok`):
  - `roam:u:<uid>`: his positions and emotes. WRITE only on your own uid, only while you are visible with a fresh presence row (`roam_pos_write`). Nobody can publish under another name; a hidden / opted-out player cannot publish at all.
  - `roam:t:<uid>`: his chat bubbles. There is NO insert policy: only `roam_say` can write here (it runs the chat filter and `realtime.send` from the database), so the text, the name and the position are trusted. A client could write a fake bubble on its own position topic, but phones only read bubbles from the text topic.
  - READ (both kinds): your own topic, or the topic of a player who is visible, not hidden, fresh (`roam.live.stale_s`), not banned, not blocked either way, and whose presence cell is within 1 cell of YOUR OWN fresh presence row, while your street session is live (not ended, not timed out). So a topic can only be joined where `roam_nearby` would list the player: guessing a uid, a far-away player, a hidden player or a blocker gives nothing, and `roam_topic_ok` (callable by players) is no oracle beyond what the list shows. Blocks are therefore enforced by the server for positions AND bubbles, both ways. Realtime checks policies on join and caches them: a block made while joined takes effect for an honest client at once (it leaves) and for a modified client at its next join or token refresh.
- **Positions are the viewer's own row, not a number he sends.** `roam_nearby` needs a live session and a presence row, takes no coordinates and is rate limited (`presence_min_s / 2`); `roam_live_tick` takes a claimed position but bounds it by what the last row allows (`roam.drive.max_speed_mps` x seconds + 20 m), so nobody can hop across the city to look at other streets. A throttled tick does no query (answers `players: null`, "no change"). Positions are returned rounded to 1 m.
- Name, look, car and badges NEVER come from a payload: they come from `profiles` (name, avatar, street cred, office title, crew badge) and the car's item id from the SERVER session.
- `roam_say`: the chat kill switch (`chat.enabled`), mute, new-account rule, word filter, the chat's duplicate window (`chat.duplicate_window_seconds`), its own gap and per-minute limit, a short log (`roam.bubble.log_hours`, 24 h). `roam_report`: only players you were near (a presence row or a bubble in the last 10 minutes, within about 3 cells of you, or your own walk is that recent), 6 an hour, snapshot of his last bubble; admins read them with `admin_roam_reports`.
- Privacy: "Show me to others in free roam" (`roam_set_show`, default on) AND the friends location privacy (`profiles.share_location_friends`). A hidden player keeps a presence row marked `hidden` (it anchors his own position, nobody else is ever shown it) and still SEES others.
- Admin switch `roam.live.enabled`: the next tick of every phone raises `off`, the client leaves every channel and shows "Live players off" (it looks again after a minute); new joins are refused at once. A modified client that ignores it keeps its already-joined channels until it rejoins.
- Tested in `roam3_test.sql` with `set role authenticated` + claims (own topic allowed; another's topic, look-alike uids, text topics, presence extension and anon refused; reads follow proximity, both-way blocks, hidden players, an ended / timed-out / stale viewer, the master switch), plus the nearby filters, the clamps, the rate limits, bubbles, reports and grants.

### Interest management (cheap)
- `roam_presence(user_id pk, cx, cy, x, y, mode, veh, hidden, updated_at)`: one upsert by `roam_live_tick` every `roam.live.presence_s` (9 s), rate limited on the server (`presence_min_s` 5 s). Nothing is written per frame. `roam_leave`, walking into a place and hailing a keke delete the row. Stale rows are removed 25 at a time by an index range scan, bubbles 50, reports 20: nothing loops over players.
- `roam_nearby` returns the nearest `max_shown` players of your 100 m cell and its 8 neighbours within `radius_m` (100 m), never hidden-location, opted-out, banned, blocked either way or stale (> 45 s). Static info is only sent for ids the phone does not hold (`p_have`).

### Position stream and cost levers
- `[x10, y10, heading, speed, flags, seq]` (flags: car, run, brake, reverse, emote), about 28 bytes. The sender uses dead reckoning: it sends only when the path leaves what the receiver predicts, or the heading / speed / flags change, or the longest gap passes; a standing player sends every 20 s (the server list refreshes his place every 9 s). Each message is delivered to every listener (cost 1 + listeners), so a per-player **message budget** (`roam.live.budget_mps`, 3) stretches the gap: 3 listeners about 1 s, 8 listeners 2.7 s.
- **One cap for joining and drawing**: the phone listens to (and draws) only the nearest `max_shown` (8; `low_max` 5 on Graphics Low) players within the radius; bubble channels are joined only for players within 35 m. The sender's budget divisor is the number of players it really draws. The sequence number starts at a random value and a track restarts its sequence after a 3 s silence, so a rejoining phone is never frozen out. A stream position more than 40 m (plus a car's speed since the last list) from where the server last listed the player is ignored, so avatars cannot teleport and the collider / bubble radius stay honest.
- Receivers (`live/track.ts`) draw 200 ms in the past, interpolate, extrapolate along heading and speed up to 5 s, and snap on a jump over 10 m or a gap over 12 s. Players scale in over 0.5 s and out over 0.8 s. Remote cars use the drive scene's body and wheels (no cockpit) in the owner's colour. Emotes (wave, nod, laugh, dance, thumbs up, come here) ride on the position flags.
- Hidden tabs leave all channels and ask for nothing; they rejoin when shown. Only problems with the phone's OWN channel (or the socket / the tick) count towards switching the layer off; a refused topic of another player (hid, blocked, left) just backs off.
- UI: a small chip ("3 nearby" / "Live players off"), two round buttons above Run (emote tray, say bar with four quick lines), name tags and bubbles as HTML over the heads, tap a tag for the player card (Wave, Add friend, Block with a confirm, Report with the chat's reasons), and the chip opens the live settings.

### Defaults (a small Realtime budget)
`roam.live.enabled` true, `budget_mps` 3, `max_shown` 8, `low_max` 5, `move_hz` 2, `idle_s` 20, `presence_s` 9, `presence_min_s` 5, `stale_s` 45, `radius_m` 100, `cell_m` 100 (keep radius at or below the cell), `buffer_ms` 200; bubbles on, 80 characters, 25 m, gap 3 s, 10 a minute.

### Collision
Other walkers are soft circles: the player and the car cannot enter them, and nothing is ever shoved (only the local body moves). A push that goes nowhere for 1.5 s makes players passable for 2.5 s (and until you stop overlapping), on each phone for its own body, so nobody can be trapped. Remote cars are solid boxes for your car but a contact is never counted as a hit (`DynBox.remote`, hit kind 3): network lag cannot damage a car. Walkers also go in the `people` list, so your car and the traffic brake early for them. `node scripts/roam-collide-check.mjs --only=6` tests all of it.

### Fail safe
Realtime in poll mode, a quota message, a dead socket or three failed ticks takes the layer down to "Live players off" and free roam carries on alone; it retries after 10 s, 20 s ... 5 min. A refused topic is not retried for 16 s, then twice as long each time. `roam.live.enabled` off = every phone's next tick stops it.

### Data and load test (local Supabase, `node scripts/roam-load.mjs --k=30 --secs=60`)
30 fake clients (real WebSockets, private channels, RPCs): 25 in one 70 m square walking random paths, 5 far away; with the new defaults each lists 8 neighbours. The numbers are in the report that came with this change (see below) and should be re-measured with the script. The earlier numbers with 20 listed and budget 6: 276 messages / 24.6 KB payload / about 38 KB on the wire received per player per minute, 12 sent. The checks the script makes: no message from an unexpected source, nothing crossing between the two areas, forged packets on a victim's topic never delivered, bubbles only from listed players, every bubble carrying the server's name, 0 channel errors.
Plan limits as I know them (check the pricing page): Free 100 msg/s, 200 connections, 2 M messages a month; Pro 500 msg/s, 500 connections, 5 M included then about 2.5 USD per extra million (the spend cap is a hard limit). The local Realtime tenant defaults to 100 msg/s and drops messages above it (`UnableToBroadcastChanges too_many_requests`); raise it in `_realtime.tenants` to test Pro-like.

### Monthly Realtime messages with the new defaults (a model, not billing data)
A moving player costs `r x (1 + n)` messages a second (r his send rate, n listeners); the budget caps it at about 3 a second whenever n is 3 or more, so a moving player is at most about 11 000 messages an hour and a mixed one (60 % moving) about 7 000.
- 100 players in free roam at once for 2 hours a day (6 000 player-hours a month), however they cluster (max 8 listed each): about 6 000 x 7 000 = **42 M messages a month**, peak about 250 to 300 a second (under the Pro limit of 500, over the Free 100). About 37 M over the Pro allowance, roughly **90 USD** a month of overage.
- If players spend only 20 % of their play time in the street, divide by 5. On the Free plan (100 msg/s, 2 M a month) the live layer saturates with about 35 moving players at once and uses the monthly allowance in about 280 player-hours: switch it off (`roam.live.enabled`) or lower `budget_mps` to 1.5 / `max_shown` to 5 while the budget is small.
- Watch Supabase Reports > Realtime for a day, then tune `budget_mps`, `max_shown`, `presence_s`.

## Limits / left for later
No walking inside buildings, no injuries or ragdolls, NPC "Talk" is a greeting line, opening hours are not checked by `roam_enter` (the place screen shows closed, as with travel). Fuel burn on the client between checkpoints is an estimate; the server's answer wins. The car is kinematic (no drifting, no body damage on the model, the camera shakes and the condition falls). The server cannot see that a car really drove the route (positions are cosmetic): it caps what a checkpoint may claim and requires the reported distance before pulling up at a place. The far backdrop has no trees. The night sky is a flat dark blue. Dev-only collider overlay: `?debug=1`.
