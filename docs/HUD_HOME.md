## 3D home
- **One canvas**, orthographic isometric camera from the south-east, `frameloop="demand"`, DPR capped at 1.5. It redraws every frame only while the Sim walks, blends between poses, a tap marker fades or the player drags; otherwise 24 fps for the idle life (12 fps asleep or under reduced motion). Paused while a sheet covers it (it only finishes a walk/blend under way), not drawn when hidden/off-screen.
- **Only one WebGL canvas on screen:** when the Sim sheet's Profile tab (turntable) or the Edit look sheet opens, `HomeView` takes a still frame (`snapshot()`), unmounts the home canvas and shows the image until the overlay closes.
- **Drag** rotates the view ±43°, pinch/wheel zooms 0.85-1.9×. A drag never counts as a tap.
- **Day/night** from the game clock (`engine/light.ts`): hemisphere + sun/moon, warm indoor point light at night, window glass and lamp shades change colour, sky gradient behind the canvas.
- **Cost:** the room is merged into 4 meshes (solid, glow, glass, screens) with vertex colours: ~3.2k-5.5k triangles, 4 draw calls. The character adds ~32-40 draw calls and 8k-16k triangles (hair and outfit decide). Measured in the game (R4 check, SwiftShader): hostel 36 calls / 14.3k tris, self-contain 43 / 19.2k, duplex 44 / 21.0k; a full frame renders in 0.2-0.8 ms (`__home.bench`, dev only).
- **No WebGL / context lost:** the home's SVG scene (`src/art/scenes/home_*.tsx`) with a "Things to do at home" button.

### Furniture sets (starter homes by origin)
Migration `supabase/migrations/20261006000400_starter_furniture.sql`, tests `supabase/tests/furniture_test.sql`.
- **Server data (Admin → Content → Furniture / Starter furniture):** `furniture` (id, name, 3D `kind`, default `slot`, `activities` it hosts, `rest_pct`, colour, active), `starter_furniture` (origin + optional `start_home` → piece + optional slot; a row for one home replaces the all-homes row in the same slot), `player_furniture` (what each player owns; select-own RLS, written only by the server).
- **Seeds:** LAPO = water drum + bucket (`bathe`), one stool (`sit_rest`), kerosene stove (`cook_home`), thin foam mattress (`sleep`/`nap`, rest 90 %). Nepo = double bed (single in the UNIBEN hostel), sofa (`relax_sofa`), TV (`watch_tv`), fridge (`cold_drink`, ₦300), gas cooker (`cook_home`), wardrobe, rug, centre table.
- **Given** by a trigger when a home is chosen (v2 creator) or a v1 profile is created; existing players were backfilled once by origin + start home (housing id for v1 Sims). Always additive: nothing is ever removed.
- **Actions:** `activities.needs_furniture` (TV, radio, sofa, fridge, stool) = offered at home only with a piece that lists it; `do_activity` refuses otherwise (hint `no_furniture`). Sleep/bath/toilet/cook never need furniture. The sleeping piece's `rest_pct` scales the energy (mat 90 % of a bed). Nobody owns a radio now, so "Listen to the radio" is hidden until a piece lists it.
- **3D (`furnishLayout` in `model.ts`):** the house keeps its fixtures (`FIXED_KINDS`: toilets, showers, bath, sink, shared bucket stall, generator, compound drum, clothesline, AC); everything else is replaced by the player's pieces, each placed at its layout `slots[slot]` ([x, z, rot]; slots: bed, seat, sofa, tv, stove, fridge, bath, wardrobe, rug, ctable). Unknown kinds or slots a layout lacks are skipped. The game loads `player_furniture` once per home (`useCatalog.loadFurniture`) and waits for it before mounting the home. New kinds: `drum_bucket`, `gas_cooker`; the stool is now a seat.
- **Where the Sim goes (`pieceFor`):** the group's explicit actor (the sofa for TV), else the piece that hosts the activity (the drum for the bucket bath), else the group's usual piece. Tapping one of your pieces lists what it hosts (a seat also lists TV/radio); fixtures and wish chips list by group. Both lists hide furniture-only actions you can't do.
- **Short actions start at once:** the Sim walks to the piece only when the walk takes at most `home.walk_max_share_pct` (15 %, admin → Settings → Action timing) of the action's remaining seconds; otherwise it is placed there immediately.
- **Dev page:** `/dev/home?l=face_me&o=lapo` (o = lapo | nepo uses a copy of the seeds; omit for the full default furnishing).

### Furniture -> activities
`ACTIVITY_GROUP` maps home activities to a group: `sleep`/`nap` -> bed, `cook_home` -> kitchen, `bathe` -> bath, `use_toilet` -> toilet, `watch_tv`/`listen_radio` -> media. Anything else is `seat`. Tapping a piece opens its group's activities (a seat also lists media; the wardrobe opens "Change your look"). When a home activity is running (`profile.busy_label` = the activity name), the Sim walks to the group's piece (`actorFor`: a piece with `actorFor: [group]` wins, e.g. the sofa or chair for TV/radio) and holds the group's pose: lie (bed), sit (sofa, chair, toilet), cook, scrub. When it ends the Sim stands up beside it (0.42 s blend back to the idle). Idle Sims wander every 16-34 s (45 s after the player moved them; never while busy). See "M1 movement".

### Add a piece of furniture
1. Add the kind to `FurnitureKind` and `KINDS` in `model.ts`: footprint `w`/`d`, `solid`, optional `group`, `spot` (where the Sim stands, local `[x, z, facing]`, +z is the front) and `seat` (sit: hips `[x, y, z, yaw]`; lie: feet `[x, y, z, yaw]`, head towards -z).
2. Build it in `buildPiece` (`engine/furniture.ts`) from `b.box()` / `b.cyl()` (bottom-centre placement, local frame). Use `{ layer: 'glow' }` for lamp shades, `'glass'` for windows/mirrors, `'screen'` for screens.
3. Place it in a layout's `furniture` (`x`, `z`, `rot` in quarter turns, optional `y` and `color`).
4. Check `/dev/home?l=<layout>` and the nav test idea in the R4 report (every actor spot reachable from the door).

### Add a home layout
Add a `HomeLayout` to `LAYOUTS` (house `w`/`d`, `lot` incl. yard, floor/patch colours, partitions with doorway gaps, low front-wall `doors`, back-wall `windows`, idle `home` spot, furniture) and map its `housing_id` in `HOUSING_LAYOUT`.

## M1 movement (2026-10-06)
Files: `src/art/sim/nav.ts` (grid + A* + smoothing, no imports), `src/art/sim/locomotion.ts` (the walker), `src/art/home3d/nav.ts` (HomeLayout -> obstacles, re-exports), `src/art/avatar3d/engine/anim.ts` (`poseLife`, `poseGait`, pose buffers; see docs/AVATAR3D.md "M1 rig animation"), `src/art/home3d/engine/HomeScene.tsx` (taps, actor, blends, frame rate), `.home3d__hint` in `src/styles/game.css`, `simPosture()` in `src/lib/mood.ts`. Check: `node scripts/nav-check.mjs` (`-v` prints the grids). **Client-side only:** where the Sim stands is never sent to the server (no RPC, no migration); the server stays authoritative for actions.
- **Tap the floor to walk:** an invisible plane over the whole lot takes the tap (r3f raycast; furniture tap boxes sit above it and win). A tap = under 8 px of movement, under 550 ms, one finger (a drag-rotate, pinch or wheel never walks). A green ring grows and fades at the end point (0.7 s, ease-out). A tap on furniture without actions, a wall or outside the reachable area walks to the nearest reachable point (`snapEnd`); a sealed pocket ends at the closest reachable cell, never through a wall.
- **Pathing (reusable for L2 places):** `makeGrid(bounds, cell)` + `blockRect` / `blockSegment` with a clearance pad, then `planPath(grid, from, to, { snapEnd, round })` -> `{ points, reachable, end, length }`. Home: 0.2 m cells, 0.12 m pad, walls minus door gaps, solid floor furniture. A* = 8 neighbours, no corner cutting, binary heap (a 200×200 maze solves in ~35 ms in node; home grids < 1 ms); then string pulling (line of sight) and corner rounding (0.22 m quadratic curves, only where they stay clear). Start/end points may sit in the pad (a bed's spot).
- **Walker (`stepWalker`):** speed follows a critically damped spring (ease in/out, no snaps), slows for the stop (v² = 2·a·d with a hard cap so it lands at walking-stop speed) and for each corner by its angle; heading eases the short way round with a turn-rate cap; a heading more than ~43° off while standing turns on the spot first (small shuffle steps), and the walk ends with an eased turn to the piece's facing. Re-targeting mid-walk keeps the speed. Cruise 1.15 m/s (trousers), at least 0.72 m/s for short-stride outfits, ×(1 − 0.18·tired). The gait phase advances by distance / stride, so the feet never slide.
- **Furniture taps:** keep their behaviour (the activity sheet opens at once) and the Sim also walks to the piece's spot if that takes ≤ 7 s, while the sheet is open. When the action starts, the walk rule is unchanged: walk if it fits in `home.walk_max_share_pct` (15 %) of the action's seconds, hurry up to 1.4× if it fits within 1.4× of that, else the Sim is placed there (blended when within 1.6 m, instant when further). Because of the pre-walk the Sim is usually already at the piece, so short actions start where they should without delay.
- **Busy rule:** while `busy_until` is in the future (`walkLock` from Game.tsx) or the Sim holds a task pose, a floor tap does not walk: a small glass pill "<activity> first, then you can walk" rises at the tap for 1.6 s. No pre-walk on furniture taps either.
- **Alive:** idle = `poseLife` (breathing ~4 s, weight shifts, look-arounds, fidgets, mood posture from `simPosture(profile)`: tired = energy < 40 or mood score < 38 (slump), happy = mood score > 66 (small bounce)). Idle↔walk is mixed by the smoothed gait weight; any state change (sit down, lie down, stand up, task end) blends 0.42 s (ease in-out) from the last frame, including the root position/rotation, so nothing snaps. Reduced motion: smaller idle, no fidgets, no pose blends, idle 12 fps.
- **Frame rate:** `Driver`: full rate while walking/blending/marker/dragging/orbiting, 24 fps idle, 12 fps asleep or reduced motion, nothing when the tab is hidden or off-screen; paused under a sheet except to finish a walk/blend.
- **Welcome back (S2):** `interactive={false}` = no floor plane, no taps; the idle life and the arrival walk still play (the WelcomeBack screen itself is unchanged and passes no mood, so the Sim stands neutral there).

Per-frame JS for the Sim is a few hundred sin/cos and two 96-float mixes.
