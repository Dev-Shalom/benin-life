## Who to approach (best fit first)
| Group | Examples | Why they'd care | What you offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Benin City businesses | lounges, restaurants, barbers, boutiques, car dealers, schools and tutorial centres in GRA, Ugbowo, Ring Road | your players live in their city | their name on an in-game billboard, a branded spot on the map, a shout-out post |

| Telcos / data | MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile | young players online all day | "Data dey flow" events, data giveaways as prizes |

| Tech programmes and grants | Edo Innovates / Edo Tech Park, Tony Elumelu Foundation, Google for Startups Africa | a local tech product with real users | equity-free grants, mentorship, office, visibility |
| Influencers and comedians | Benin and Naija skit makers, gaming streamers | fresh content, a game about their city | free in-game fame (title, billboard), revenue share on referrals |
| Schools / student groups | UNIBEN, AAU, Igbinedion student unions, tech clubs | students are the core players | campus tournaments, election-style events |

Avoid betting and alcohol brands: many players are young and it can get the app flagged.

## What a sponsor gets (your offer menu)
- **In-game billboard** (already built): their logo/ad on the 3D city and map. 7 days ₦150k, 3 days ₦75k, 1 day ₦30k (adjust).
- **Branded place or item**: a lounge, food or product with their name.
- **Sponsored event**: club night, election week, "money spray" event with their name.
- **Push + in-game announcement** to every player.
- **Social posts**: TikTok, Instagram, X with their brand.
- **Prizes**: they give data / airtime / vouchers, players compete for them.

### Tips so it sounds like you
- Change the "why you fit" line for every company. One real sentence beats a long letter.
- Send to a real person (marketing manager, brand manager, owner), not info@.
- Attach 2–3 screenshots or the 30-second trailer link. Don't attach big files.
- Follow up once after 4–5 days: "Hi [Name], just bumping this. Happy to send a short video of the game instead."
