# Family & Love (stage 1 and 2)

Owner: "There should be a way that players can try to ask other person out. When they accept, if they flirt to the person, they should have a flirting level. When it gets to 30% that is high, then the two of them are together. It shows in the Family app. They can do all sorts of things like in real life together: go out together, go to certain places together, have some fun together. Stage 2 (kids) is below."

Migration `supabase/migrations/20261026000200_family_love.sql`, test `supabase/tests/love_test.sql`. Client: `src/screens/game/phone/FamilyApp.tsx` + `FamilyApp.css`, `src/api/love.ts`, `src/screens/game/LoveAskButton.tsx` (the "Ask out" button on a player's card), admin `src/admin/Love.tsx`. Help article `family-love`, tour step `family`.

One phone icon, **Family** 💞, three tabs: **Link-ups** · **Us** · **Family**. Deep links: `go=family` (Link-ups), `go=family:us`, `go=family:family`, `go=family:linkups`.

Everything is server-authoritative and lazy (no cron). Clients never read or write the tables: RLS is on, there is no grant and no policy; every action is a security-definer RPC.

## The rules as built (every number is a Setting, category `love`)
1. **Opt-in.** `profiles.love_open` (default OFF) gates being asked and being listed. Switching it off withdraws the asks you sent and drops the asks you got.
2. **Ask out** `love_ask(target)`. Needs: `love.enabled`; both opted in; both accounts at least `love.min_account_days` (2) old; neither at `love.max_partners` (1; talking and couple both count); no block either way (the existing chat block list, `bl_social_blocked`); the caller under `love.asks_per_day` (5, rolling 24 h, every ask counts); not within `love.decline_cooldown_days` (3) of a decline by that person; not within the break-up cooldowns; not yourself; no pending ask either way. The target gets an alert (`love_ask`) and a push (category `friends`). A pending ask expires after `love.ask_expire_hours` (48). **Declining is silent**: the asker sees "No reply" (an expired ask shows the same). Accepting makes a relationship in status `talking`, tells the asker, and ends every other open ask of anybody now at their limit. `love_withdraw(ask)` takes back an ask you sent.
3. **Flirt level** (one shared integer 0-100 per pair). `love_flirt(rel, flirt)` sends a line from the `love_flirts` menu (no free text): price (ledger `love_flirt`, a sink), points added, `min_level` to unlock, cooldown per sender `love.flirt_cooldown_minutes` (20), and a daily cap per pair `love.flirt_daily_cap` (20 points per game day, flirts and dates together). At the cap the flirt is refused and costs nothing; with only a little room left it adds what fits and still costs the full price. The partner gets an alert + push (pushes for one chat fold into "3 new flirts for you"). **Decay** is lazy: after `love.decay_after_days` (3) idle days (no flirt, no date) the level loses `love.decay_per_day` (2) a day. Reading computes the shown level without writing; the next write (flirt, date) stores it, for that pair only. A couple never falls under the couple level (they stay a couple); a talking pair can fall to 0.
4. **Couple.** The flirt (or date) that takes the level to `love.couple_level` (75 since 20261026000300; was 30) flips the status to `couple`: both get a `love_couple` alert + push ("You are now a couple 💞"), a `couple` row goes in the pair's log. The level keeps counting to 100 as "bond". Profiles show "💞 With @name" (a talking pair shows "💬 Talking to @name") unless the player turns `profiles.show_relationship` off (default on) in the Family tab. `get_public_profile` gained `relationship` and `love_open`.
5. **Dates** `love_date_invite(date, rel?)` then `love_date_respond(invite, accept)`. `love_dates` rows: place (a real `locations` id, or empty = on the phone), `cost_each`, `fun_gain`, `social_gain`, `bond_points`, `cooldown_hours` (per pair per date), `min_status` (`talking` or `couple`). The inviter must stand at the place when inviting; **both** must be there when the partner accepts (and neither jailed, in hospital or travelling). On accept each pays `cost_each` (ledger `love_date`, a sink, all or nothing: either of them short of money refuses the whole date), each gets Fun and Social (`bl_adjust_needs`), the pair gets `bond_points` (inside the daily cap; with no room left the date still happens and says "bond capped for today"). An invite waits `love.date_invite_expire_minutes` (30); one pending invite per pair; expiry is lazy.
6. **Break-up** `love_breakup(rel?)`: either partner, any time. Status `ended` with `ended_at` and `ended_by`, history kept, pending date invites cancelled, the partner told kindly. Both are blocked from asking each other for `love.breakup_cooldown_days` (3) and from asking anybody for `love.breakup_global_cooldown_hours` (24).
7. **Safety.** `love_report(target, reason)` (needs an ask or a relationship between the two; one open report per pair; `love.report_daily_cap` 5 a day; text cleaned, max 300) lists in Admin -> Love; the app offers "Also block them" (the existing `chat_block`). `love.enabled` is the kill switch (existing couples are kept). Flirt lines and date names are admin data, refused by a trigger when the chat banned-words list would mask them, and have length checks. No explicit content anywhere.

## Settings (Admin -> Settings -> Family & love)
`love.enabled, love.min_account_days, love.max_partners, love.asks_per_day, love.decline_cooldown_days, love.ask_expire_hours, love.flirt_daily_cap, love.flirt_cooldown_minutes, love.decay_after_days, love.decay_per_day, love.couple_level, love.date_invite_expire_minutes, love.breakup_cooldown_days, love.breakup_global_cooldown_hours, love.list_size (page size, max 50), love.online_minutes ("Online now" filter), love.poll_seconds (Family app refresh, 45), love.report_daily_cap`. Each has a label and description.

## RPCs (all `security definer`, `set search_path = public`, granted to `authenticated` only)
Players: `love_overview()` (one read: me, incoming / outgoing asks, my relationships with log, flirt and date menus, the rules the UI quotes), `love_candidates(filter all|here|online, offset)` (paged, at most 50 a page, indexed, public info only: no money, no location), `love_set_open`, `love_set_show_relationship`, `love_ask`, `love_respond`, `love_withdraw`, `love_flirt`, `love_date_invite`, `love_date_respond`, `love_breakup`, `love_report`. Admin: `admin_love_overview`, `admin_love_report_set`, `admin_love_end` (audited). Private helpers `bl_love_*` are revoked from clients.

## Ledger and economy
Reasons `love_flirt` and `love_date`: pure sinks, never earned (`bl_earn_reasons()` drops them even if an admin lists them), labelled in `bl_ledger_label`, category "Shops, food and fun" in `bl_econ_category`. Nobody is ever paid for a flirt or a date.

## Alerts (events table, `bl_event`)
New kinds: `love_ask, love_accept, love_couple, love_flirt, love_date_invite, love_date, love_breakup`. Push goes through the existing `friends` category (`bl_push_enqueue`), links `/play?go=family:linkups|us|family`. The Alerts list shows an "Open Family" button; the Family icon shows a badge for unread `love_*` alerts.

## Performance
The Family app polls `love_overview()` only while it is open (`startPoller`: visible tab, not AFK, backs off when idle) every `love.poll_seconds`, and again at once when a `love_*` alert arrives. The Link-ups list is loaded on open, filter change or after an action, never polled. No Realtime subscription was added. Every list is capped (page at most 50, 20 asks, 15 log rows, 5 dates in "recent"). The list filter uses `profiles_love_open_idx` (partial, `last_seen desc`) and the partial relationship indexes. Decay only touches the pair being read or written.

## Admin
Settings -> Family & love; Content -> **Flirt lines** and **Dates** (text fields have length limits, shown in the form and checked in the table); **Love** page (open players, talking, couples, ended in 7 days, asks waiting, open reports, a report inbox with Reviewed / Dismiss and "End link-up", audited as `love_report_*` / `love_end`). Mute or ban a player in Players.

## Stage 2: kids (built)
Owner: "They should be able to give birth in the game. Like cater for the needs of the child. There should be times where the child needs attention that will alert the person that: the child needs attention and you need to be there for the child."

Migration `supabase/migrations/20261027000100_family_kids.sql`, test `supabase/tests/kids_test.sql`. Client: `src/screens/game/phone/KidsSection.tsx` (the Kids part of the Family tab), `familyUtil.tsx`, `src/api/kids.ts`, `src/state/kids.ts`; the Family icon dot is in `Phone.tsx`. Master switch `kids.enabled` plus `love.enabled`; every number is a Setting in category `kids` (Admin -> Settings -> Kids, 39 keys with labels and descriptions).

### The rules as built
1. **Having a baby.** `kids_try(rel?)` then `kids_accept(invite, accept)` (`kids_withdraw` takes it back). Only a `couple` (not talking) that has been a couple at least `kids.min_couple_days` (2), under `kids.max_children_per_couple` (3) and `kids.max_children_per_player` (5) active children each (expected, home or with relatives; grown and gone do not count), with no pregnancy running. One partner proposes (and needs the money), the other has `kids.invite_expire_hours` (24) to say yes; the proposer waits `kids.invite_cooldown_hours` (6) before asking again. On yes each pays `kids.try_cost_each` (5,000, ledger `kid_try`, a sink; all or nothing, the answer says who is short). A "not yet" costs nothing and the asker sees "would like to wait". The pair is then "Expecting a little one" for `kids.pregnancy_days` (3), shown to both with a countdown. A break-up while an invite waits cancels it.
2. **Birth** is lazy: the due time is the child's `next_alert_at`, so the alert sweep gives birth within a minute, or the first `kids_status()` read after the due time does. Both parents get "Your baby is here! 👶". `kids_name(child, name, gender)`: 3 to 16 letters, tidied to "Osaze", refused when `bl_chat_mask` would mask it (the same banned-words list as love and chat), the first parent to save wins, the other is told. Boy or Girl only changes the look. After `kids.name_grace_hours` (24) the game picks from Content -> Baby names (`kids_auto_names`). The child belongs to both parents and stays linked after a break-up.
3. **Life stages** in `kids_stages` (Content -> Child stages): Baby 0, Toddler 3, Child 8, Teen 14 (day the stage starts, emoji, and the need rates). At `kids.grow_days` (21) the child grows up and moves out: keepsake alert, both parents get the cosmetic **Proud parent** badge (`get_public_profile.proud_parent`, shown only when "Show my relationship" is on). No money, no XP.
4. **Needs**: hunger, sleep, fun, health, love, 0-100. Each is stored as value + its own timestamp and computed on read as value minus rate times elapsed hours, adding each stage's rate for the time spent in it. Nothing is written until a care action. `kids_care(child, feed|sleep|play|medicine|cuddle)`: restores `kids.<action>_restore`; feed (`kids.feed_cost` 400) and medicine (`kids.medicine_cost` 1,500) cost money (ledger `kid_care`, a sink); every action has a per-child cooldown shared by both parents (`kids.<action>_cooldown_minutes`: 10, 30, 30, 60, 30); refused for free when the need is already at `kids.care_max` (90); medicine only when health is under `kids.medicine_below` (60) or the child is sick. Either parent can care, the app shows who did what.
5. **Needs-you alerts.** A child "needs you" when a need is under `kids.alert_below` (30) (or is sick). `kids_children.next_alert_at` (indexed, `kids_children_due`) holds the next moment the sweep must look at the child: the first need crossing the level (respecting the cooldown and quiet hours), the birth, a name deadline, sickness, relatives, growing up. It is recomputed after every care action and every read that settles the child. `bl_kids_alert_sweep()` takes at most `kids.sweep_batch` (200) due children (`for update skip locked`), settles them, and sends an event plus a push (category Friends) to both parents: "🍼 Osaze needs attention: hungry. Go and take care of them." At most one alert per child per `kids.alert_cooldown_hours` (3); after `kids.max_alerts_unanswered` (4) unanswered alerts it goes quiet until somebody cares; optional quiet hours (`kids.quiet_enabled`, from/to hour) move the alert to the end of the window. The in-app side is `kids_status()`: it settles only the caller's own due children (at most 20 rows) and returns everything the Family tab needs, including `attention` (the red dot count). It is polled only while the phone is open (`startPoller`: visible, not AFK, slower when idle, `kids.poll_seconds` 45) and once after every action or `kids_*` alert. `get_my_state` is unchanged.
6. **Neglect, gently.** A need at 0 for `kids.neglect_hours` (24) makes the child sick; medicine and a cuddle (either parent) cure them, and they cannot fall sick again for `kids.sick_grace_hours` (6). If neither parent does any care action for `kids.removal_days` (7), relatives take the child in (`with_relatives`, both told kindly). `kids_reclaim(child)` brings them home once after `kids.reclaim_wait_hours` (12) for `kids.reclaim_cost` (10,000, ledger `kid_reclaim`, a sink), needs set to `kids.reclaim_need` (60). A second removal is for good (`gone`, reason `relatives`). The word "died" is never used.
7. **Safety.** Names are moderated; nothing can trade or sell a child; switching `love_open` off does not remove a child. A parent who starts a new life (or whose account is deleted) hands the child to the other parent (alert `kids_handover`), or to relatives when none is left (`gone`, `no_parents`); an expected baby is cancelled kindly. Admin: Love page -> Kids (counts, the 50 newest active children, **Remove**, audited `kids_remove`, both parents told). `kids.enabled` off: no new babies, no care, naming or reclaim, no alerts, and every clock is paused; when it is switched back on all timestamps are shifted by the pause so nobody is punished (trigger on `game_config`, table `kids_pause`). `love.enabled` off only blocks new babies.
8. **Performance.** Lists are at most 20 rows, indexed by parent (`kids_children_a`, `kids_children_b`) and `kids_children_due`; no cron per child; the sweep is batched; no new Realtime subscription; the care log keeps the last 40 rows per child.

### Scheduling: what the owner must set up
The sweep rides on the scheduler the push pipeline already has: `bl_push_tick()` (called by the Edge Function `player-push`) now ends with `bl_kids_alert_sweep()`. The pushes it queues are claimed in the same run. **Nothing new to create if the `player-push` Cron job from docs/NOTIFICATIONS.md is already set up** (Supabase -> Integrations -> Cron -> Edge Function `player-push`, every 1 minute, header `Authorization: Bearer <anon key>`). If it is not, set that job up now: without it the tick only runs when some player's game pokes `player-push` (at most once a minute, after a DM, money, friend request...), so alerts and births are late when nobody is active. Everything else still works without Cron: births, sickness and relatives are settled on read, and the open phone shows "needs you" from `kids_status()`. Admin -> Love shows "Due for the sweep"; it should sit near 0.

## Decisions to confirm with the owner
- A couple does not fall back to "talking" when the level decays; it only loses bond down to the couple level.
- The Link-ups list shows a name, face, street cred, office title, gang tag, "Online" and "Same place". No wealth tier badge (it is derived from money).
- An ask counts toward the daily limit even when withdrawn, declined or expired.
- Dates need the inviter at the place when inviting and both at the place when accepting.
